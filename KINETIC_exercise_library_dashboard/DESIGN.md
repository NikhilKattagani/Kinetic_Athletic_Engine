## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-performance physical culture through ultra-crisp editorial precision, modern industrial restraint, and tactical clarity. Designed for athletes, coaches, and dedicated strength practitioners, it avoids decorative workout tropes, aggressive skeuomorphism, and generic fitness gamification. Instead, it positions the muscle exercise library as a scientific, precision-calibrated athletic dashboard.

The aesthetic fuses **Minimalist Engineering** with **High-Contrast Performance Details**:
- Crisp structured panels on an airy, balanced canvas.
- Razor-sharp typographic hierarchy utilizing geometric and humanist grotesques.
- High-visibility functional accents (Electric Lime and anatomical system codes) that guide muscle targeting and exercise taxonomy with zero ambiguity.
- Tactile, deliberate interactions optimized for intense focus, rapid catalog scanning, and structured workout programming.

## Colors

The color architecture is built upon a high-contrast athletic foundation: high-energy Electric Lime paired with deep structural charcoals, grounded over a clinical off-white backdrop.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base**: `#F6F7F4` (Soft Off-White) provides low eye-strain contrast against indoor training lighting.
- **Card & Modal Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
- **Surface Outline / Divider**: `#DDE2DC` (Mist Gray) for razor-thin, structural separation.
- **Primary Text & Headings**: `#171A1F` (Deep Charcoal) provides impenetrable structural legibility.
- **Secondary Text & Meta**: `#5C6470` (Muted Slate) for tertiary metadata, sets, and cadence.
- **Surface Dark / Inverted Element**: `#171A1F` paired with `#252A32` (Graphite) for headers, athletic metrics, and active states.

### Brand Accents
- **Electric Lime (`#C6F36B`)**: Primary interaction point, active state highlight, and workout execution triggers.
- **Lime Accent Tint (`#EAF6D1`)**: Repetition badge base, active metric backing, and pill fills.
- **Lime Accent Deep (`#31541A`)**: Repetition typography, active icon outlines, and high-legibility lime text.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the bold, industrial curves of **Manrope** for impact headings, metrics, and structural titles with the neutral, hyper-legible matrix of **Inter** for descriptions, tags, execution mechanics, and data tables.

- Headings utilize tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to project condensed power and athletic density.
- Technical meta labels, muscle group names, and workout protocol tags use uppercase small styling (`label-sm`) with positive tracking (`0.05em`) for glanceable reading on gym floors.
- Numerics and repetition values within cards utilize `stat-badge` with bold tabular weights to eliminate jitter across workout trackers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around a flexible 12-column grid system (4 columns on mobile, 8 on tablet, 12 on desktop) designed to showcase the Exercise Card grid with balanced density.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Flow
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: Max container `1520px` with 2rem side margins and 1.5rem gutters. Exercise cards reflow in a 3-column or 4-column module.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column layout with 1.5rem side margins. Exercise cards reflow into a 2-column or 3-column module.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4-column layout with 1rem safe margin. Exercise cards display in single-column stacks with edge-to-edge illustration preview.

Spacing follows an 8-point system scaled down to 4px units for tight metric badges and data-dense athletic summaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on low-contrast structural outlines paired with light, diffuse ambient grounding. Heavy drop shadows and glossy blurs are omitted to preserve crisp tactical execution.

- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas)**: `#F6F7F4` matte foundation.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Module)**: `#FFFFFF` encased in a 1px solid `#DDE2DC` outline. Resting shadow: `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(23, 26, 31, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(23, 26, 31, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Hover & Active Drag)**: Card lifts subtly with `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(23, 26, 31, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(23, 26, 31, 0.03)` with border shifting to `#171A1F`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Flyouts, Filter Drawers, & Modals)**: `#FFFFFF` with `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(23, 26, 31, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains unified, athletic geometry centered around a defined 16px radius for core modules:
- **Exercise Cards**: Fixed `16px` border radius (`rounded-lg`), delivering structural solidity.
- **Illustration Containers**: Internal top corners inherit the `16px` card curvature or maintain an internal nested `12px` radius.
- **Action Buttons & Chips**: `8px` (`rounded-md`) for controls and filtering pills to preserve operational precision.
- **Badges & Tags**: Full circular pill rounding (`9999px`) for reps, sets, and muscle identifiers to contrast against the rectangular grid.

## Components

### Exercise Library Card
- **Frame**: White surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#DDE2DC`, 16px corner radius, overflowing hidden.
- **Media Demonstration**: 4:3 fixed aspect ratio container showcasing vector anatomical demonstrations or high-contrast athletic imagery. Light gray backing `#F1F3EE`.
- **Muscle Target Indicator**: Discrete 3mm vertical accent bar or chip pinned to the top left of the illustration rendered in the corresponding muscle color (e.g., Abs & Core `#329A91`).
- **Repetition Badge**: Pinned top-right or anchored below title. Background `#EAF6D1`, text and icon in `#31541A`, weight 800, padding `4px 10px`, pill shape.
- **Card Content**: Exercise title in Manrope 18px SemiBold `#171A1F`. Equipment and secondary target tags set in Inter 12px `#5C6470`.

### Buttons
- **Primary Athletic Button**: Electric Lime `#C6F36B` background, `#171A1F` Deep Charcoal text, 8px radius, bold tracking. Hover state shifts to saturated `#B8E85A`.
- **Secondary Dark Button**: `#171A1F` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover shifts to `#252A32`.
- **Outline Button**: `#FFFFFF` background, 1.5px border `#DDE2DC`, `#171A1F` text. Active state border sharpens to `#171A1F`.

### Muscle Group Filter Chips
- **Resting State**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#DDE2DC`, text `#5C6470`. Left dot (6px) filled with the assigned muscle accent color.
- **Selected State**: Background `#171A1F`, border 1px solid `#171A1F`, text `#FFFFFF`, with muscle dot glowing or primary lime check icon.

### Form Inputs & Search Bar
- **Search Header**: Large tactile input, 12px radius, pure white surface with 1.5px `#DDE2DC` border. Icon left in `#171A1F`. Focus ring transitions to 1.5px solid `#171A1F` with a soft `0 0 0 3px rgba(198, 243, 107, 0.45)` focus ring.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes**: 20x20px square with 4px corner radius. Unchecked: 1.5px `#DDE2DC` on white. Checked: Deep Charcoal `#171A1F` fill with Electric Lime `#C6F36B` check icon.
- **Segmented Control**: Matte Off-White `#F1F3EE` trough with 8px radius. Active segment transitions with Pure White `#FFFFFF` card, subtle elevation, and `#171A1F` label.
